[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-cover-art-01/kornztt/gta6-cover-art/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01)

A photo and a caption, made into a multi-panel poster cover — offline, in your
desktop browser, with the photo never leaving your machine.

## What it does

- **Multi-panel layouts**: Hero (one big photo), Split (two panels), Grid (three
  photos plus a title card) and Strip (three bands).
- **Colour grades**: Sunset, Neon, Dusk and Mono, applied as washes and scrims
  over the photos, with a striped retro sun disc.
- **Any photo**: upload your own, or use the two bundled samples. Click a panel
  to pick it, drag the photo to crop it, zoom with the slider, recenter any time.
- **Words**: a big bold title and a subtitle, top or bottom, that shrink to fit.
- **Fast to a PNG**: one click exports the cover at Square 1080 x 1080, Portrait
  1080 x 1350 or Story 1080 x 1920.

## Try it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

## Build the entry

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This is an entry for the [make.fun gta6-cover-art-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-cover-art-01).
All artwork is drawn in code; no third-party images, logos or fonts are used.
